The vehicle

1,500 kg dry, one 7,000 N throttleable engine at 311 s specific impulse (close to the Apollo descent engine). Every newton burns propellant at ṁ = T / (Isp · g₀), so the lander gets lighter and more responsive as it descends.

How the autopilot flies

It picks a target descent rate that shrinks with altitude and a sideways speed that shrinks with distance to the pad. The gap between target and actual becomes a desired acceleration, which sets the tilt angle and throttle each frame.

The program modes

The labels copy Apollo: P63 braking burn from high altitude, P64 approach once the site is in view, and P66 for the final vertical descent. Pressing P mid-flight is the same takeover Armstrong made in P66.
